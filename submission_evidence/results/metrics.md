# Measured localization performance

## Interpretation

These measurements use a fresh fixed-seed synthetic FinFET pool generated by the repository's frozen public generator and labels from its corrected centre-rule contract. They demonstrate measured consistency with that synthetic contract; they do **not** establish accuracy on independently labelled real SEM imagery.
