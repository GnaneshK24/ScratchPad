# References

These sources motivate qualitative image effects and the classical
registration operations used here. They do **not** claim that the synthetic
generator's numerical parameters are calibrated to a particular SEM tool.

## SEM imaging effects

1. D. C. Joy, “Noise in secondary electron emission: the low yield case,”
   *Journal of Microscopy*, 54(4), 361–367 (2005).
   [DOI: 10.1093/jmicro/dfi044](https://doi.org/10.1093/jmicro/dfi044).
2. S. Ando *et al.*, “Special raster scanning for reduction of charging
   effects in scanning electron microscopy,” *Scanning*, 36, 327–333 (2014).
   [DOI: 10.1002/sca.21112](https://doi.org/10.1002/sca.21112).
3. J. D. James and E. F. M. W. de Jong, “Scanning Electron Microscopy,”
   *Methods in Molecular Biology* (2012).
   [Open-access article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3352184/).

## Classical localization and registration

1. OpenCV, “Template Matching.”
   [OpenCV documentation](https://docs.opencv.org/4.12.0/de/da9/tutorial_template_matching.html).
2. G. D. Evangelidis and E. Z. Psarakis, “Parametric Image Alignment Using
   Enhanced Correlation Coefficient Maximization,” *IEEE TPAMI*, 30(10),
   1858–1865 (2008).
   [DOI: 10.1109/TPAMI.2008.113](https://doi.org/10.1109/TPAMI.2008.113).
3. OpenCV, `findTransformECC` API documentation.
   [OpenCV documentation](https://docs.opencv.org/master/dc/d6b/group__video__track.html).
